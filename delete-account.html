Delete your CricCorner account

You can delete your CricCorner account and the personal data linked to it in either of these ways.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open CricCorner and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom, type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email criccornersupport@gmail.com from the email address you registered with (or tell us the email and mobile number on the account). We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, profile, profile photo, mobile number and email address
  • The name and contact details on your own player entry (the entry becomes “Deleted player”)
  • Your name and contact details on any issue or suggestion you sent us

What is kept

  • Match scorecards, scores and team lists, so other players' records and statistics stay correct. Your name on them is replaced with “Deleted player”.
  • Teams and matches you created for other players remain available to them.
  • Deleted data can stay in routine backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

See our Privacy Policy for more.
