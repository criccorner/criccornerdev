CricCorner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

CricCorner (“the app”) is a cricket team and scoring app. It is provided by Ashutosh Bam and Cric Corner (“we”, “us”), based in YOUR_COUNTRY. You can contact us at criccornerdevdb@gmail.com. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs or messages. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. How we use information

To run the app: sign you in, keep your teams, matches and statistics, sync them between your devices and your teammates, respond to support requests, keep the service secure and prevent misuse. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other app users. Cricket information is shared inside the app: your name, role, profile photo, the teams you belong to, and the matches and statistics you take part in can be seen by other CricCorner users. Your email address and mobile number are hidden (masked) from other users in the app. Administrators of the app can see them.
  • Service providers. We use Supabase to host the database, file storage and sign-in. If you use Google or Apple sign-in, that provider handles the sign-in step. They process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Where information is stored and how it is protected

Data is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored in a database hosted by Supabase. A copy of your data is also kept on your device so the app works offline. Access to the database is controlled by security rules and accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account (see below) your login, profile, photo, mobile number and email are deleted, and your name on the player entry is replaced with “Deleted player”. Match records are kept so that other players' scorecards and statistics stay correct. Deleted data may remain in routine backups for a short period before it is overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your details in Profile → Edit Profile.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. You can also request deletion on our account deletion page.
  • Other requests (a copy of your information, correction, objection): email criccornerdevdb@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time (normally within 30 days).

Depending on where you live (for example under Australian privacy law or the GDPR), you may have additional rights, including to complain to your local privacy regulator.

7. Children

CricCorner is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent or guardian who believes a child has registered can email us and we will delete the account. Junior players may be added to a team by a parent, guardian or coach, who is responsible for having permission to do so.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
